📍 Location during office hours

To keep attendance fair for everyone, the same location check applies to every employee:

  • Your phone records its location only while you are punched in — from punch-in until punch-out, and never after 7:30 PM.
  • Nothing is recorded before you punch in, after you punch out, on holidays or on leave.
  • The office sees the places recorded during office hours, and you can see how many checks were made today.

⚙️ Leave policy

The same rules for everyone. Casual leave is earned every month and unused days carry forward until the leave year ends; vacation and medical leave are yearly. Everything starts fresh at the start of the next leave year.

Extra days for one person

Added to (or taken from) the policy for that person, every year until you change it. Leave at 0 for most people.
